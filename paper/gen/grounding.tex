Over 10 platforms (396,123 quasi-steady samples from 5,327 one-minute segments), the fitted understeer gradients range from -0.31 to 2.81\,deg/g (median 1.77; per-platform 95\% CIs by cluster bootstrap over segments), and the fitted steering ratios from 11.9 to 18.1. The simulator's dry-road value, from the same fit on 60 steady turns, is 2.01\,deg/g (fitted ratio 0.997; it should be 1). This is inside the real range: within the 95\% CI of 2 platforms, above it for 5 and below it for 3 (Figure~\ref{fig:grounding}). Two platforms (\v{S}koda Octavia, Audi A3) give near-zero or negative gradients together with the highest fitted ratios. A speed- or angle-dependent steering ratio would break the linear model there, so their values are shown but not interpreted. This checks one linear-range property of the model (the regime before the drop), not its limit behaviour on low friction.
